Clinical Workflow

How we coordinate with your clinic from impression capture to delivery.

1

Capture Scan / Impression

Send digital STL files via our portal or request physical impression pickup from your clinic in Lahore.

2

CAD Modeling / Waxing

Our technicians calibrate emergence profiles, occlusal contacts, and margin line seals.

3

Milling & Layering

Restorations are milled or cast, sintered, hand-layered with ceramics, and bench-checked on dies.

4

Rider Delivery to Clinic

Cases arrive disinfected, labeled, and sealed in clinical presentation boxes ready for seating.
